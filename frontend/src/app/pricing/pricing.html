Questions about trust

What happens if I stop paying?

Your codes keep working through a grace period, then fall back to a URL you choose — nothing is held hostage. And because the core is open source, you can always self-host for free and keep every code alive.

Does the trial charge me?

No. The 14-day trial needs no credit card. You only add payment if you decide to continue after the trial.

Can I self-host instead of subscribing?

Always. Tessera is MIT-licensed. Run it on your own server with unlimited codes at no cost — the hosted plans just save you the ops work.

Where is my data?

On the hosted plans we run in the EU. Visitor IPs are never stored — they're resolved to a country in flight and discarded. The scans table has no IP column by design.
